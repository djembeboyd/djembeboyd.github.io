WorkspaceMCP

WorkspaceMCP is a personal tool used by a single individual on their own computer. It connects that person's own Google accounts (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Tasks, Contacts, Chat and Apps Script) to a local AI assistant, so the assistant can read and organize the owner's own mail, files and schedule on request.

It is not offered to the public. No other users sign in to it.
